Privacy

Effective October 2, 2026 · Dayloom Studio

Aeterna has no app account, advertising, analytics, developer server, tracking, or remote crash reporting. We do not receive your cards, dates, photographs, search history or backup files.

On-device data

Records and preferences are stored in a private database. Selected personal photos and thumbnails are copied to private local storage. Calendar conversion, search, image processing and backup encryption happen on your device. Photo and document pickers access only items you select.

Widgets use bounded local display snapshots and can show your information on a home or lock screen. Consider who can see your device. The widget extension has no access to the full database or original photographs.

Purchases

Buying or restoring Pro connects to Apple App Store or Google Play and uses that store's account and policies. The app does not receive your payment card details. StoreKit verifies Apple transactions; Google Play purchase signatures are verified locally, and signed purchase proof is cached privately, outside user content backups. No purchase data is sent to Dayloom Studio. Apple and Google purchases are separate.

Your control

You can edit and delete records, restore deleted records through deletion history, or permanently erase local app data in Settings. Erasing data does not refund or cancel a store purchase; use the store's purchase controls. Exported backups remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately. Password-protected backups use encryption; keep the password safe.

Private app files and widget snapshots are excluded from automatic OS backup. Use Aeterna's explicit backup and restore to transfer content. Uninstalling may remove local records; export a backup first. Restoring content never grants Pro purchase rights.

Support and changes

If you voluntarily contact us through support, your message is handled by the service you choose. Do not post private records or purchase tokens publicly. Material changes will be reflected on this page and in the app.

隐私政策

Aeterna 无需 App 账号，无广告、统计、追踪、开发者服务器或远程崩溃上传。卡片、日期、照片、搜索及备份在设备本地处理，我们不会收到这些内容。系统照片与文件选择器只访问你选定的项目。

小组件使用本地显示快照，可能在主屏幕或锁屏上显示信息，请留意设备的可见性。小组件无法读取完整数据库或原始照片。

购买和恢复 Pro 会连接 Apple App Store 或 Google Play；支付资料由商店处理，App 不接收银行卡信息。Apple 交易由 StoreKit 验证，Google Play 签名在本地验证，签名凭证仅在私有目录缓存，不进入用户备份，也不发送给 Dayloom Studio。两家商店的购买权益独立。

你可编辑、删除记录，通过删除历史恢复，或在设置中永久清除本地数据。清除数据不会退款，退款需通过商店处理。已导出的备份需自行删除。密码备份使用加密，请妥善保管密码。私有数据排除自动系统备份，迁移请主动导出备份；卸载可能删除本地记录。恢复内容不包含付费权益。

如主动联系支持，消息由所选服务处理。请勿公开私人记录或购买凭证。政策变化会更新到本页和 App。

如主動聯絡支援，訊息由所選服務處理。請勿公開私人記錄或購買憑證。政策變更會更新到本頁和 App。
