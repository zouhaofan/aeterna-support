# Aeterna · Dayloom Studio

A quiet place for the days that matter.

This repository hosts Aeterna's public support website and privacy policy.
The app's source code is maintained separately.

- [Website](https://zouhaofan.github.io/aeterna-support/)
- [Privacy policy](https://zouhaofan.github.io/aeterna-support/privacy.html)
- [Support and purchase restoration](https://zouhaofan.github.io/aeterna-support/support.html)
- [Report a problem](https://github.com/zouhaofan/aeterna-support/issues/new)

English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese are included. Reports are
public; do not attach private records, photos, backup passwords or purchase tokens.

Free Lite + a planned US $1.99 one-time Pro upgrade. No ads. No subscription.
The final local price and availability will be displayed by Apple App Store or
Google Play when the app is released. No store listing is represented as live yet.
