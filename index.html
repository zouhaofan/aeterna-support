A quiet place for the days that matter.

Keep anniversaries and countdowns together in personal cards, with photos, gentle themes and home-screen widgets. Records and backups stay on your device.

Free Lite. One-time Pro.

Lite includes three cards, three dates and three personal photos per card, black and white themes, selected artwork, and one widget card. Local levels and daily XP progress, one fixed Quiz sample, default home Large and timeline Medium sizes, standard contrast, search, editing, deletion history, and backup and restore are included. Both editions have no ads.

Pro unlocks all themes and artwork, multiple widget cards, the full Quiz, personal profiles, adjustable home and timeline card sizes and interface contrast, and removes product-imposed limits. The planned US base price is $1.99, purchased once through Apple App Store or Google Play; your store displays the local price. There is no subscription. Apple and Google purchases are separate.
